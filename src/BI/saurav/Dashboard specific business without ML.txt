# Final Business Health Score             
 Business Health Score = ([Rating Score] * 0.50 +[Review Score] * 0.30 +[Checkin Score] * 0.20 )


#Health Categories
Business Health Status = SWITCH(TRUE(),[Business Health Score] >= 80, "Excellent", [Business Health Score] >= 60, "Good",[Business Health Score] >= 40, "Average", "Needs Improvement")
___________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

#Customer Engagement Score
Customer Engagement Score =([Review Score] * 0.60 +[Checkin Score] * 0.40)

#Engagement Status
Customer Engagement Status =
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    [Customer Engagement Score] >= 80, "High",
    [Customer Engagement Score] >= 60, "Moderate",
    [Customer Engagement Score] >= 40, "Low",
    "Very Low"
)

For a polished Power BI dashboard focused on a single business,

KPI Cards: Business Health Score, Customer Satisfaction Score, Customer Engagement Score, Popularity Score
Gauges: Business Health Score, Customer Satisfaction Score
Line Charts: Review Trend, Rating Trend
Bar Charts: Monthly Reviews, Monthly Check-ins, Tips by Month
Scatter Plot: Reviews vs. Check-ins (or Reviews vs. Tips)
Donut Chart: Open vs. Closed (if comparing multiple businesses)
Detail Table: Business name, city, category, stars, reviews, health score, engagement score
